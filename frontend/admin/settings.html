Manual Email Reminders Control Panel

Manually trigger system-wide email sweeps. These run live checks against student timetables and thresholds, queueing outbound reminder notifications immediately.

Daily Check-In Reminders Sweep
Checks today's timetable schedule for all departments. Queues warning emails to students who have missed or skipped marking any active periods today.
Low Attendance Warnings Sweep
Calculates live overall attendance ratios. Queues alerts to students whose total average has fallen below their configured target thresholds (e.g. 80%).
Attendance Summary Broadcast Sweep
Calculate and queue the periodic attendance summary emails for a specific date range. Emails are compiled using the winking-envelope HTML template.
