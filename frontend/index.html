About Trackify & Application Purpose

Trackify is a personal attendance management and digital campus platform designed specifically for college students. It enables students to easily log daily class attendance, view department timetables, calculate safe lecture bunks to maintain 80%+ attendance eligibility, and receive automated schedule notifications.

Google Account Integration: Trackify offers Google Sign-In so students can securely log in or register using their institutional Google email. We only request basic profile access (Name and Email) for authentication. Your data is strictly private and never shared with third parties.

TRACKIFY ATTENDANCE

Math-backed confidence before every skipped class

Instead of guessing if you can take Friday off, Trackify uses exact fraction logic:
Attendance = (Attended + SafeBunk) / TotalClasses.

It tells you the exact count — e.g. "You can bunk 3 more classes and stay at 81.2%" or "Attend the next 2 classes to reach 80.0%".
