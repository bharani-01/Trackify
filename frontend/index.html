Official Overview & Google OAuth 2.0 Integration Policy

Application Purpose

Trackify is an automated student attendance tracking and timetable management web application. It enables college students to log daily lecture attendance, monitor subject-wise percentages, calculate safe lecture bunks to maintain 80%+ eligibility, and organize academic schedules.

Google Sign-In & OAuth Scope Usage

Trackify integrates Google OAuth 2.0 Sign-In to allow students to securely register and log in. We request access strictly to basic profile details (Name and Email) to authenticate accounts, save personal timetables, and sync attendance records securely across devices.

Student data is encrypted, kept confidential, and never sold or shared with third parties.
