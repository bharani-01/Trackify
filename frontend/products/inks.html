The Reality Check

No More Last-Minute Print Shop Stress

Submitting lab manuals and project reports before the morning bell shouldn't mean a 45-minute fight at the campus stationery shop.

The Traditional Xerox Queue
  • ✕
    40-minute morning queues: Crowding around shop counters before 9:00 AM class, risking being marked late.
  • ✕
    Pendrive virus infections: Plugging your personal USB drive into public shop computers ridden with shortcut malware.
  • ✕
    Formatting shifts & lost fonts: Word documents opening with shifted tables and misaligned margins on ancient PCs.
  • ✕
    Payment friction: Cellular dead zones inside basement xerox shops causing failed UPI transactions.
The Inks Digital Cloud Experience
  • ✓
    Zero waiting in line: Upload assignments from your hostel room bed at night or on your commute to campus.
  • ✓
    100% pendrive-free: Direct cloud upload with encrypted ephemeral storage that auto-purges after collection.
  • ✓
    Font & margin lock: Server-side rendering guarantees that what you see on your screen is exactly what prints.
  • ✓
    10-second counter pickup: Show your token code or scan the pickup QR, grab your hot stack, and walk to class.
